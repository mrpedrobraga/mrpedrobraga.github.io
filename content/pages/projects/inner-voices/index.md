**Greetings.**

You do not know me, but soon we will become familiarised.  
Or rather, you will become familiarized with my creation(s).

_"Inner Voices."_

I have been planning it for a long, long time; now I begin to craft this amazing _thing_ that _took over my life._

_Can you hear the chirp of the birds in this quiet morning?_  

You will feel happy and smile.  
You will feel pain.  
You will feel warm and comforted.  
You will feel cold and alone.  
  
The _coward_, The _estranged_,  
The _rebel_, The _denier_,  
The _thinker_, The _hopeless_,  
The _helpless_, The _unimportant_.

Soon you will meet their _worlds_.  

Once _it_ is not occluded by the darkness of your eyelids.

I wait for you, my friend.  
**Together** we will create _something really, truly, extraordinary._

_Soon._  

**Pedro Braga.**
